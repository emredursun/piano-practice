# Outreach Templates — Phase 0

> **Volume:** 25 messages across 3 customer-discovery segments + 15 messages to pedagogues. Day 1–2 of Week 1.
> **Authority:** `../../decisions/PHASE0-SETUP.md §6, §8`; `/plan` WS2-T03, WS3-T01.
> **Tone:** See [`../../design/UX-COPY.md §1`](../../design/UX-COPY.md) — respectful, specific, calm. Not salesy.

All templates are **starting points**. Personalise name + reference to something specific you know about the recipient (their YouTube channel, their ABRSM chapter, a mutual contact). Generic templates land in spam or get ignored.

## T-08 — Pedagogue Consultation Brief (1-page PDF attached to T-06/T-07)

### Piano-Practice — Pedagogue Consultation Brief

**Who's asking.** <Founder Name>, solo founder, building an early-stage browser-based piano practice product for serious adult amateurs. Based in <City>, TR.

**What the product is (v1.0).**
A PWA for adult pianists (16+). Real engraved notation via OSMD. Sampled Steinway (Salamander V3) via Tone.js. Real MIDI input. Score-follower with chord aggregation. AI fingering suggestions trained only on public-domain editions (Czerny 1840, Busoni 1894, Parncutt 1997 algorithm). **15 Bach Two-Part Inventions** only in v1.0. $29 one-time lifetime license. Geo: TR + EU only.

**Three questions I'd like to cover in the hour:**

1. **Licensing existing curriculum for 1 grade level.** Approximate all-in cost (upfront + per-user royalty) and typical timeline for a commercial browser-based product to license a grade-1 or grade-2 piano curriculum from a reputable publisher (Faber, Hoffman, ABRSM, RCM, or similar). Representative deal structures.
2. **Authoring from scratch for 1 grade level.** Your professional estimate on the effort (hours / months) and fee for a certified pedagogue to author a 1-grade piano curriculum from scratch, commercial-use-licensed to my company. Realistic, not aspirational.
3. **Bach-niche viability.** Your professional opinion on whether a 15-piece Bach-only corpus, pedagogically well-executed (honest fingering, clean engraving, score-following, FSRS-seeded spaced repetition), could stand on its own as a respectable product for serious adult amateurs — vs. what they'd consider "a bigger catalog needed".

**Reference material I'll share before the call:**
- 60-second demo of the prototype (WebM).
- A sample AI-vs-editorial fingering layer on BWV 772 (PNG).
- My own short bio + credentials.

**Logistics.**
- 1 hour, video call via Whereby.
- €120 paid via Wise within 2 business days, against your invoice.
- No follow-up obligation; I may ask one follow-up email clarifying a number.

**Confidentiality.**
I'm happy to sign a simple NDA if you prefer. Otherwise, I will not publish your name or quotes without your explicit separate permission.
